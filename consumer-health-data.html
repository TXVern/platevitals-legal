Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — PlateVitals

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Applies to: the PlateVitals Android app (com.vern.diettracker) and anyone who uses it, with specific rights for residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (SB 370, NRS 603A.400–.550) and Connecticut (Conn. Gen. Stat. §42-515 et seq., consumer health data provisions).
Business: Vernon Bearss, doing business as PlateVitals · Contact: csavern@gmail.com
Main Privacy Policy: https://txvern.github.io/platevitals-legal/

“Consumer health data” means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and identifies your past, present or future physical health status. This page lists exactly what PlateVitals collects of that kind, why, where it comes from, who receives it and how to use your rights.

1. Consumer health data we collect and why

Category Examples Why we collect it
Health Connect readings Steps, active calories burned, heart rate, weight, blood oxygen (oxygen saturation), blood glucose Show them on your dashboard, Trends and goal rings
Phone step sensor Daily step count Steps when Health Connect steps are not available
Readings you type Glucose readings, weight, waist Show your log, averages and history
Wellness goals you choose Blood Sugar Awareness / Low Carb, Low Sodium, Heart-Healthy Eating, Weight Loss & Portion Control Adjust your suggested calorie, sugar and sodium targets
Body and diet details Age, sex, height, activity level, diet style, calorie and nutrient goals Calculate your daily targets
Food and water logs Meals with estimated nutrition, meal photos you choose to scan, water Food log, history and estimates
Consent record Date and time you agreed and confirmed you are 18 or older Show that you consented

We collect only what is needed to provide the features you ask for. We do not collect precise location and do not use geofencing.

2. Where it comes from

  • You, when you type it or choose options in the App.
  • Android Health Connect, only for the six types you allow; that data was written there by your other apps and devices (for example Abbott Lingo, Samsung Health, a watch or a ring).
  • Your phone's step sensor, if you allow Physical activity access.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the App's features you request: your dashboard, goals, history, Trends, food estimates, reminders and backup to your account. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not use it to make decisions about credit, insurance, housing or employment.

4. Who receives it (sharing)

We never sell consumer health data. It is disclosed only to these service providers (processors), which act on our behalf:

Third party Consumer health data it receives Purpose
Google LLC — Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore Your account details and the backup of your profile, wellness goals, meals, glucose, steps, active calories, weight and heart rate (only if you sign in) Sign-in and backup
Google LLC — Gemini API Meal photos or food text you choose to analyze (no health readings) Nutrition estimate. PlateVitals currently uses Gemini's free service, under which Google may use submissions to improve its products.

Barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts are not consumer health data. PlateVitals has no affiliates, and no third party collects consumer health data from the App across other websites or apps over time.

5. Consent

Before you first connect Health Connect, pick a wellness goal or enter a glucose reading, the App asks for your consent to collect and use your consumer health data as described here, and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. You can say “Not now” and keep using the rest of the App. We will ask again before collecting consumer health data for any new purpose or sharing it with anyone new.

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can: - Confirm and access: ask whether we collect your consumer health data and get a copy of it, plus a list of every third party and affiliate that received it and how to contact them. - Review and correct: see and change your data in the App at any time, or ask us to correct it. - Withdraw consent: disconnect Health Connect in the App or in Health Connect settings, turn off Physical activity access, or delete your account. Withdrawing stops further collection. - Delete: delete your account and all its data in the App (Settings → Account → Delete account), or by email (below). Deletion covers our service providers and backups we control; we tell our processors to delete it too. - Appeal: if we deny your request, you can appeal (section 7).

We will not treat you differently or charge you more for using these rights.

7. How to make a request or appeal

  • Email: csavern@gmail.com, subject “Consumer health data request” (or “PlateVitals account deletion”), from the email you use with PlateVitals so we can verify it is you.
  • In the App: Settings → Account → Delete account (deletion), Disconnect (Health Connect).

We confirm receipt within 7 days and answer within 45 days. If we need more time (up to 45 more days) we will tell you why within the first 45 days. Requests are free up to twice a year.
Appeals: reply to our answer with the subject “Appeal”. We will decide within 45 days and explain our decision in writing. Washington residents who are unhappy with the result may contact the Washington State Attorney General (https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint); Connecticut residents may contact the Connecticut Attorney General (https://portal.ct.gov/ag); Nevada residents may contact the Nevada Attorney General (https://ag.nv.gov).

8. Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts Firebase data at rest. Access is limited to the account owner. We keep consumer health data while your account exists, or on your phone until you delete it, and delete it when you delete your account (email requests within 30 days).

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new effective date and show a notice in the App before a material change takes effect. If a change would collect or share consumer health data in a new way, we will ask for your consent first.
